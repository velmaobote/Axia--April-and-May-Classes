Letter to My Future Self

Dear 3-months-from-now me, Right now, I’m deep in the code trenches, learning, breaking things, fixing them, and sometimes fixing what was never broken in the first place. But this letter isn’t just a checkpoint. It’s a celebration and a promise. A celebration of how far I’ve come, because not too long ago, I didn’t even know what a <div> was. I couldn’t center a button to save my life. I thought position: absolute was out to get me.
But now? Now I speak in rems and components and flex like it’s a second language. It hasn’t just been about the tech. It’s been about consistency. About opening VS Code even when I wanted to close everything and binge-watch comfort shows like yet another season of The Real Housewives I’ve already seen twice but “need” for emotional research. It’s been about doing the hard things even when they weren’t glamorous. And most of all, it’s about reminding myself daily that there will only be time for leisure if I get the hard things done efficiently. It hasn’t just been about code.
It’s been about showing up for myself. Opening my laptop even when I wanted to throw it across the room. Debugging things line by line. Being patient when the layout didn’t layout. It’s been about doing the hard things consistently, even when it wasn’t cute or aesthetic. Because I’ve learned something important: there will only be time for leisure if I get the hard things done efficiently.
And July, my birth month, my beginning, is more than a date. It’s my declaration. This month, I’m not just celebrating; I’m committing. I’m committing to learning front-end development. To becoming the kind of person who finishes what she starts. Who stumbles, who Googles, who asks questions, and keeps building anyway. I want this July energy to echo through every line of code I write. I want it to carry me when motivation fades.
July, oh, sweet July, isn’t just another month. It’s my month. My lucky start. My annual reset. And I’ve chosen to commit in it. To code. To clarity. To becoming the version of me who finishes what she starts. Who stumbles and keeps going. Who reads error messages like they’re riddles and celebrates console.log like confetti.
While I work, I’m also soundtracking my growth with the voices of my fellow Cancers. Luke Bryan reminds me to keep it grounded. Selena Gomez keeps it gentle and strong. Lana Del Rey whispers nostalgia and softness into the background. Solange Knowles brings that calm, creative elegance. Ariana Grande powers through with vocal runs and resilience. Tate McRae feels what I feel. Camila Mendes channels quiet strength. Chloe Bailey brings the soul and self-assurance. Missy Elliott is my hype woman. Post Malone gives chaos and chill. Ringo Starr adds rhythm. Jessica Simpson reminds me that growth is allowed to evolve. Lil’ Kim says don’t be small. Kali Uchis is all vibe and vision. Priyanka Chopra Jonas is the global force. And Julianne Hough? She moves with intention, and I want that too.
So, future me, if you’re reading this: I hope you kept showing up. I hope you’ve built something you’re proud of. Helped someone with your knowledge. I hope you’re still curious. Still learning. Still dancing to Lana and Ariana when CSS won’t cooperate. Because this isn’t just about learning to code. It’s about becoming someone who trusts herself. Someone who doesn’t run from the hard stuff. Someone who codes, commits, and continues.
With love and a Cancer season playlist, present-day me
